About Inkwell

A quiet desk for loud thoughts. Inkwell turns your stylus, finger or mouse into six writing instruments, lays them on four kinds of paper, and remembers how you wrote — not just what.

How your pages are kept

Every stroke is saved the moment you lift the pen — as vectors (position, pressure, tilt and timing), not pixels — into IndexedDB, a database built into your browser. That is why any page can be replayed with Watch me write. Export a .inkwell file to move pages between devices, or PNG / SVG to share them. Nothing ever leaves your device.

Pens & devices

  • Stylus (Apple Pencil, Wacom, S Pen, Surface Pen): true pressure and tilt via Pointer Events.
  • Mouse or finger: pressure is simulated from speed — slow down for weight, speed up for hairlines.
  • Stylus only mode in the tray ignores your palm.

Keyboard

  • 1–8 pens & tools
  • [ ] nib size
  • Ctrl/⌘+Z undo, +Shift redo
  • P watch me write
  • Esc stop / cancel stamp
